Explainable AI · Computer vision data · Dashboard

PIPVIS: Explaining AI Predictions of Pedestrian Behaviour

Autonomous vehicles rely on AI to predict whether a pedestrian will cross, but these models are hard to debug. With SYSTRA UK & Ireland and transport AI researchers, I built an award-winning interactive dashboard that explains a model's decisions using six hours of dashcam footage (about 300,000 labelled frames and 1,842 pedestrians).

The model scored 91.4% on validation data, and the dashboard showed why the rest failed: in several missed crossings, the pose estimator had locked onto a nearby occluding pedestrian. The team now has a concrete fix to test. Awarded Highly Commended Early Career Researcher at Digital Footprints 2026.

Data engineering · Sustainability analytics · Dashboard

Sustainable Food Sales Explorer: Seasonal Footprint of UK Food Purchasing

Every shopping basket carries a carbon, land and water footprint, but how that changes through the year had never been measured at this level of detail. I built a Python pipeline linking 160 million product sales from a major UK retailer to environmental impact data, and a D3.js dashboard that lets researchers explore weekly footprints across up to 65 food categories, with overlays for holidays, cultural events, school terms, heatwaves and sporting events.

Our festive analysis found emissions from food sales rose 15.3% in December. Per-kilogram emissions also rose 6.6%, showing that the mix of products changed, not just the amount bought.

Dashboard shown with dummy data; the live version will be hosted by the Healthy and Sustainable Places Data Service.
